# Audit 2026-10-24 — rules × 9.1 million A-share daily bars

Before Marina was published, its China A-share price-limit records were checked against the maintainers' private history: **5,460 securities (including 236 delisted), 9,139,186 daily bars, 2017–2026**, using the method now shipped as `scripts/audit_bars.py`. The timeline was also compared record-by-record with the independently compiled chronology in `data-backtest-live-common` 0.3.0.

## Result

No record in this release is contradicted by the data where the data can speak. Specifically:

| record | bars checked | violations |
|---|---|---|
| `cn.limit.main_board.1996-12-16` (post-2017 bars) | ~6.4M | 0 beyond tolerance* |
| `cn.limit.chinext.2009-10-30` → `2020-08-24` | ~1.3M | 0 across the switch date |
| `cn.limit.star.2019-07-22` | ~0.6M | 0 |

\* A known 0.44% of main-board rows in the source dataset carry out-of-band values traceable to a vendor adjustment artefact, not to the rule; they are excluded as data defects.

## What the audit found about the *data* (not the rules)

The audit is symmetric: it flags whichever side is wrong. Three defects surfaced in the maintainers' own dataset and are recorded here because anyone auditing their own bars will likely hit the same ones:

1. **ST status stored as a static flag.** The dataset's `isST` column was back-filled with "is ST *today*" across all history, and stopped updating in 2024-02. Consequence: 20 of 1,339 post-2024-03 simulated buys were in fact ST on the buy date. Rule tables cannot fix this — ST is a point-in-time *list*, out of Marina's scope — but the audit exposes it immediately.
2. **Newly listed shares.** 6,869 bar-days fell in "no limit" windows (first 5 days post-2023-02-17 main board, post-2020-08-24 ChiNext, STAR); 350 of them moved beyond the regular band. Engines that apply the regular band to IPO days mis-flag these as bad data.
3. **Rounding.** On 52,618 of 8.85M bar-days (0.59%) the limit price computed with Python's banker's `round()` differs by CNY 0.01 from the exchange's half-up rounding. `marina_rules.round_half_up` exists for this reason.

## Verification status at release

All 21 records were tagged `unverified` at v2026.10.24. The primary-source audit completed the same day (`sources-audit-2026-10-24.md`) raised all records to `verified` and corrected four of them; tag `2026.10.24b` carries the result.
