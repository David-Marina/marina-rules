# E-0003 — the standard bonus-share formula breaks on reorganisation conversions

**Symptom.** A series of unadjusted prices rebuilt from an event table (or adjustment factors built
from one) shows, on every day before a capital-reserve conversion, prices far outside the daily band
of the prices actually traded — by the same factor every day — and a gap on the ex-date that did not
happen. `sh.600715` (*ST, 5% band), conversion of about 11.88773 shares per 10, ex-date 2024-12-16:
previous close 2.45; the standard formula gives 2.45 / (1 + 1.188773) = **1.12**; the exchange's
reference price that day (`preclose`) was **2.12**, and the stock closed at 2.23, exactly the limit
price round_half_up(2.12 × 1.05, 2). Against 1.12 that close is +99%. Factor 2.12 / 1.12 = 1.893.

**Cause.** The ex-rights reference price replaces the previous close as the basis of the day's price
limit — SSE trading rules art. 4.3.3: 「按除权（息）参考价格作为计算涨跌幅度的基准，本所另有规定的除外」; SZSE art. 4.4.2 the same,
both in force since 2006-07-01. The standard formula is
「除权（息）参考价格＝[（前收盘价格-现金红利）+配（新）股价格×流通股份变动比例]÷（1+流通股份变动比例）」 (SSE art. 4.3.2),
but the same articles let the issuer ask for a different one: SSE 「本所可以根据申请决定调整除权（息）参考价格计算公式，并予以公布」;
SZSE (since 2011-02-28) 「经本所同意的，证券发行人应当向市场公布该次除权(息)适用的除权(息)参考价计算公式」. In bankruptcy
reorganisations the converted shares go to investors and creditors for cash or debt, not to holders, and the
issuer routinely uses its own formula — often conditional: no adjustment at all if the record-date close
is at or below the average conversion price. SSE's reorganisation guideline (第13号, 2022-03-31, revised
2025-03-14) art. 39 says what an adjusted formula must take into account and adds
「权益调整方案约定的转增股份价格高于上市公司股票价格的，可以不对上市公司股票作除权（息）处理」. Formulas differ between
issuers (601005 in 2017 and 002506 in 2014 used different ones).

**The fact that settles it.** 文投控股 (600715), 「关于重整计划资本公积金转增股本事项实施的公告」, 2024-12-10, §2 and §4:
1,854,853,500 shares before; 2,205,000,000 converted (ratio 1.188773), none to existing holders —
1,000,000,000 to investors for ¥1,066,000,000 and 1,205,000,000 to creditors at ¥2.50 (¥3,012,500,000);
average price 1.85, adjustment only if the record-date close is above it. Announced formula:
(2.45 × 1,854,853,500 + 1,066,000,000 + 3,012,500,000) / 4,059,853,500 = 2.1239 → **2.12**, the exchange's
preclose. Second sample: 景峰医药 (000908), announcement of the same title, 2026-03-04, §2 and §4: 879,774,351
shares before, 879,774,351 converted (10 per 10), all to investors for ¥2,060,857,594.18 (average 2.34);
ex-date 2026-03-11: previous close 6.37, standard formula 3.19, announced formula 4.36, exchange preclose
**4.36** (factor 1.367).

**Detector.** `python errata/E-0003/reproduce.py <unadjusted.csv> <ex_date> <ratio>` — prints previous
close, exchange reference price, standard-formula price and the factor; exit 1 if they differ by more
than ¥0.01.

**What to do.** On ex-dates take the exchange's `preclose` as the reference price; do not recompute it.
When building adjustment factors from an event table, treat reorganisation conversions as needing the
announced formula (factor = exchange preclose / previous close). Check any reconstructed series against
traded prices: `marina-check` on fills priced from the rebuilt series with the exchange's `prev_close`
(or the reverse) flags each day before the event, and the ex-date itself, as `PRICE_OUTSIDE_BAND`.
